## 4. What they have that you don't — the gaps to close

Ranked by impact:

1. **Google Business Profile (GBP)** — 🔴 CRITICAL & FREE. Every local competitor has one with reviews. This is the single biggest lever for "web design sydney/[suburb]" and Google Maps. You have none. *This alone can outrank on-page SEO for local intent.*
2. **Reviews / testimonials with names + star ratings** — competitors show real Google reviews. Yours are placeholders. Real social proof = trust = rankings + conversions.
3. **Content depth** — their cost guide is 4–5× longer than yours with more sub-topics. Depth wins.
4. **Backlinks / brand mentions** — they're linked from directories, guest posts, local listings. You have ~0. In 2026 Google treats **brand mentions** (even unlinked) as authority signals.
5. **Domain age & history** — the one thing you can't shortcut. Only time fixes this. Everything else above accelerates it.
6. **Freshness cadence** — top pages are updated monthly. One-and-done content decays.
