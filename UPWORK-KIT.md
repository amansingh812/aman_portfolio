## 5. THE PROPOSAL FRAMEWORK (how to win)
Most freelancers send generic junk. You'll win by:
1. **First line = about THEM, not you.** Reference their exact project.
2. Show you understood the problem.
3. One relevant proof point.
4. One smart question (starts a conversation).
5. Short. 5–7 sentences max.

### Reusable template
> Hi [Name],
>
> [One line about their specific project + the outcome they want]. I can definitely help with this.
>
> I recently built [most relevant example] — [one-line result], so [why that's relevant to their job].
>
> Here's how I'd approach yours: [1–2 sentence plan]. You'd get a live preview link to follow progress, clean code you own, and a fixed quote up front.
>
> One question: [smart, specific question about their project]?
>
> Happy to share more — when works for a quick chat?
> — Aman

### Example A — "Need a website for my landscaping business"
> Hi James,
>
> A clean, mobile-first site that gets you quote requests from local customers — that's exactly what I build for service businesses. Happy to help.
>
> I recently built a site for a plumbing company with a quote-request form, click-to-call, and local SEO so it ranks in their area — the same setup works perfectly for landscaping.
>
> For yours I'd do a fast 4–5 page site (services, gallery, quote form, reviews) with Google Maps and a "request a quote" flow front and center. You'd get a live preview link and a fixed quote — no hourly surprises.
>
> One question: do you want online quote requests, or mainly calls? That changes how I design the homepage.
>
> When's a good time for a quick chat? — Aman

### Example B — "Shopify store setup / customization"
> Hi Sarah,
>
> Getting your store live, fast, and actually converting — not just installed — is what I focus on. I can help.
>
> I've built e-commerce stores with Stripe checkout, custom product pages, and AI product recommendations (one client saw +20% engagement after launch), so Shopify customization is right in my lane.
>
> For your store I'd set up the theme, customize the product and checkout flow for conversions, and make sure it's fast on mobile. Live preview link + fixed quote included.
>
> Quick question: roughly how many products are you launching with, and is the brand/design ready or do you need help there too?
>
> Happy to jump on a quick call whenever suits — Aman
