## 1. Google Business Profile — do this FIRST (free, biggest local lever)

1. Go to google.com/business → **Add your business**.
2. Choose **"Service-area business"** (you don't need a public storefront address).
3. Category: **Website designer** (add secondary: Web developer, Marketing agency).
4. Service areas: Sydney, Melbourne, Brisbane, Perth, Adelaide.
5. Add: logo, cover image, hours, services with prices, 5–10 portfolio photos, the phone + website above.
6. Write a keyword-rich "from the business" description (I can draft this — just ask).
7. Verify (Google will post a code or use video verification).
8. **After each job, ask the client to leave a Google review.** Reviews are the #1 local ranking factor. Target 1–2/month.

> This single profile can outrank on-page SEO for "web designer near me" searches. Nothing else here beats it.

> In 2026 Google counts **unlinked brand mentions** as authority. Getting "Build First Site" mentioned anywhere helps, link or not.

## 5. Cold outreach templates

### A. Cold email — business with a weak/no website (lead with the free audit)

**Subject:** Quick idea for {Business Name}'s website

> Hi {First Name},
>
> I came across {Business Name} while looking at {trade/industry} in {suburb} — you clearly do great work, but I noticed your website {isn't mobile-friendly / loads slowly / is a Facebook page only}, which is probably costing you enquiries from people Googling you.
>
> I build fast, lead-focused websites for Australian small businesses (fixed price, you own the code). I'd be happy to record a **free 5-minute audit** of your current site — no pitch, just the 3 quickest wins. Want me to send it over?
>
> Cheers,
> Aman — Build First Site
> buildfirstsite.com · +61 413 146 498

### B. LinkedIn connection note (300 char limit)

> Hi {First Name} — I build websites for AU small businesses and liked what {Business} is doing in {industry}. Happy to send a free 5-min audit of your site if useful. Either way, glad to connect.

### C. Follow-up (3–4 days later, if no reply)

> Hi {First Name}, just floating this back up. I put together a quick example of what I'd change on {Business}'s site — want me to send the 2-minute video? No worries if not.

### D. Referral request (after delivering a project)

> Hi {Client}, so glad you're happy with the site! Two quick favours if you have 2 minutes: (1) a short Google review here → {GBP link}, and (2) if you know one other business owner who needs a website, I'd love an intro. I'll look after them well.

### E. Guest-post / mention pitch (to local blogs / business networks)

> Hi {Name}, I write plain-English guides on websites for Australian small businesses (e.g. "How much does a website cost in Australia"). Would a guest article or a quote from me be useful for {publication/newsletter}? Happy to write something original for your audience.
