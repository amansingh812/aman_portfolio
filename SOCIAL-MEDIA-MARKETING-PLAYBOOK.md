# Social Media Marketing Playbook — Build First Site (Australia)
How to sell buildfirstsite.com online, for a developer with zero marketing background.
Researched July 2026 with current Australian numbers.

---

## First, the honest truth (read this before spending a dollar)
For a brand-new web studio, **social media is a slow-burn brand play, not a fast client tap.** The data backs this up: content marketing + personal branding take **3–6 months** to produce clients, while **cold outreach and referrals bring conversations in 2–4 weeks.** So run social media *in the background* while outbound does the heavy lifting early on. Don't quit your outbound to post reels.

Two modes, both needed:
- **FREE / Organic** — costs time, compounds slowly, builds trust. Start here.
- **PAID / Ads** — costs money, works fast *if* your funnel converts. Only after your contact form captures leads + tracks conversions.

---

## Which platforms — and how to split your effort
You sell to **businesses** (B2B) + **local Australian owners**. Research-backed allocation for a web studio:

- **LinkedIn — 60% of your effort.** 80% of B2B leads from social come from LinkedIn. This is where decision-makers are. Your #1 platform.
- **Instagram — 25%.** Visual proof (before/afters, site reveals). Reaches small local businesses, cafés, tradies, agencies. A strong "link in bio" can do most of a homepage's job.
- **Facebook — 10%.** Where AU local business owners actually are; best for local groups + paid ads later.
- **TikTok / X / YouTube Shorts — 5%.** Optional. Repurpose your other content here.

Skip trying to be everywhere. **LinkedIn + Instagram, done consistently, beats 5 half-dead accounts.**

# PART 1 — FREE (organic) channels

### 1. LinkedIn (your biggest free lever)
- **Profile** (mostly done): headline should say what you do for clients, not your title. ✅ You've got this.
- **Post 3×/week.** Types that work: before/after of a build, a short case study, "3 things I learned shipping to 10M users," a tip ("your website's first 5 seconds"). I write these for you.
- **Engage before you pitch:** like/comment on 2–3 posts from a target business owner *before* sending a connection request. Warms them up.
- **Connection notes:** always add a one-line reason ("Hi [Name], love what [Company] is building — would be great to connect").
- **DM outreach:** after connecting + engaging, send a soft, specific message (never a hard pitch first).

### 2. Instagram (free)
- Post **site reveals, before/afters, short build reels.** Visual = credibility.
- **Link in bio** → point to buildfirstsite.com (or a Linktree with Portfolio / Book a Call / Services). Many freelancers run their whole first year off this.
- Use **local hashtags** (#sydneysmallbusiness, #melbournebusiness) + geotags.
- **Reels** get the most reach — a 15-sec "watch me rebuild a bad website" clip travels far.

### 3. Facebook (free)
- **Join local AU business groups** (e.g., "Sydney Small Business," "Melbourne Tradies," suburb community groups) and *help* — answer website questions, don't spam. This alone lands clients.
- Set up a **Facebook Page** for Build First Site (needed for ads later anyway).

### 4. Google Business Profile (free — do this now)
- Not "social," but it's the highest-ROI free channel for AU local. Service-area business, collect ★ reviews. Shows on Maps.

### 5. Directories & communities (free)
- Clutch, DesignRush, GoodFirms (agency review sites that rank).
- Reddit r/webdev, r/perth/r/sydney business threads — answer, don't sell.

# PART 2 — PAID (ads) channels — Australia 2026 numbers

⚠️ **Do NOT run paid ads until:** (1) your contact form captures leads to email, (2) conversion tracking is installed, and (3) you have 1–2 reviews/testimonials. Otherwise you're paying to send strangers to a page that can't convert them.

### Meta Ads (Facebook + Instagram) — best for local AU
Current 2026 Australian benchmarks:
- **CPC (cost per click):** ~AU$1.75 average (range AU$0.50–$3.20)
- **CPM (per 1,000 views):** AU$8–18
- **CPL (cost per lead):** averaged ~AU$44 in 2025, spiking to ~AU$60 early 2026. Trades-adjacent leads run cheaper (AU$15–40).
- **Realistic starting budget:** AU$30–50/day per ad set; most small businesses see traction at **AU$1,500–2,500/month.**
- **Best use for you:** target small-business owners in specific cities (Sydney, Melbourne, Brisbane) with a "Get a free website audit" or "See our work" offer.

### Google Ads (Search) — best for high-intent
- People searching "web designer Sydney" are ready to buy — higher intent than social, but higher CPC in competitive categories.
- Use **phrase/exact match** + negative keywords ("free," "jobs," "course," "template").
- Target **specific cities**, not all of Australia (cheaper + higher intent).
- Same rule: conversion tracking first.

### LinkedIn Ads — skip for now
Powerful B2B targeting but **expensive** (high cost per click/lead). Not worth it at your stage — use LinkedIn *organically* instead.

### Paid tools worth a small spend (optional)
- **Canva Pro** (~AU$18/mo) — makes your posts look pro. Worth it.
- **Buffer / Later** free tier — schedule a week of posts in one sitting.
- **Meta verified / boosting** — only boost a post that already performs organically.

## What to improve on your site to convert the traffic (highest impact first)
1. **Add testimonials/reviews.** Biggest missing trust element. Even 2–3 quotes lift conversions more than anything else.
2. **Make the contact form capture real leads** (Web3Forms key — nearly done) + a thank-you state. Without this, ads waste money.
3. **Add a low-friction offer:** "Free 5-minute website audit" or "Free homepage mockup." Turns cold visitors into leads.
4. **Show live demo links prominently** (you have these — good).
5. **Speed + mobile** (already strong on Vite/Vercel — keep it).
6. **A dedicated city landing page** per target ("Web Design Sydney") — you've started this in your sitemap. Great for both SEO and ad landing pages.

## How to LEARN this (free, structured — ~2 weeks of evenings)
You don't need a course. In order:
1. **Meta Blueprint** (free) — free.facebook.com/business/learn — official ads training.
2. **Google Skillshop** (free) — Google Ads certification, official.
3. **HubSpot Academy** (free) — Social Media + Inbound Marketing courses, genuinely good.
4. **YouTube:** search "Facebook ads for beginners 2026," "LinkedIn for freelancers." Watch 3–4, stop researching, start doing.
5. **Just start posting.** You learn 10× faster by publishing badly for a month than by studying. AI (me) removes the writing barrier — you only need to hit "post."

## Your realistic 90-day plan
**Weeks 1–4 (free only):**
- Set up: LinkedIn (done), Instagram business account + link in bio, Facebook Page, Google Business Profile.
- Post 3×/week on LinkedIn, 2×/week on Instagram (I write all of it).
- Join 3 AU Facebook business groups; answer questions.
- Keep doing outbound + Upwork — that's what pays the bills now.

**Weeks 5–8:**
- Add testimonials as they come in.
- Repurpose LinkedIn posts → Instagram carousels.
- Launch a "free website audit" offer as your lead magnet.

**Weeks 9–12 (add paid, small):**
- Once the form + tracking + a couple reviews are live: start **AU$20–30/day Meta ads** targeting one city with the audit offer.
- Measure cost per lead. Scale only what converts.

**Free first (LinkedIn + Instagram + Google Business + local FB groups), paid later (small Meta budget once your funnel converts). Post weekly, help in groups, capture every lead — and let AI write it all so you just press publish.**
