FAQ

Questions about real estate website design

Do I need my own website if I'm already on REA and Domain?

Yes — and here's why. REA and Domain list everyone. Your own website is where buyers and vendors research YOU specifically — your results, your personality, your suburb knowledge. It's also where you capture appraisal leads directly, without REA taking a cut of the enquiry or competing agents' listings appearing alongside yours.

Can you connect my website to my real estate CRM?

Yes. We can integrate with common AU real estate CRMs including Rex, Console Cloud, PropertyMe, and Agentbox — either via API or by pulling listings automatically from REA/Domain feeds. Custom integrations are priced on request based on the complexity of your setup.

Can you build a website for a property development project?

Yes, and we enjoy it. Project marketing sites are among our favourite builds — high-impact visuals, floor plan downloads, staged EOI releases, and a registration form connected to your CRM. Get in touch and we'll put together a scope tailored to your project's timeline and release strategy.

What's the difference between an agent website and an agency website?

An agent website is your personal brand — standalone from your agency. Great for top performers who want to market themselves, not just the agency brand. An agency website represents the whole business — team profiles, all current listings, and multiple contact points. We build both, and we build them so they can work together.
