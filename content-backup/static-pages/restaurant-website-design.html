The Problem

Why most restaurant websites lose bookings

85% of people look up a restaurant online before visiting. A slow, hard-to-navigate website sends them straight to your competitor down the street.

📄

Menu as a PDF (the biggest mistake)

PDFs are slow to load, impossible to read on phones, and invisible to Google. Your menu should be a proper webpage — searchable, fast, and bookmarkable.

📸

Stock photos instead of your actual food

People eat with their eyes first. A homepage with real food photography converts dramatically better than generic stock images. We help you display your photos at their best.

🗓️

No reservation option

If someone visits your site at 11pm deciding where to go Saturday night and there's no way to book, you've lost them. We integrate OpenTable, Resy, or a simple booking form.

🕐

Opening hours not updated

Nothing worse than driving to a restaurant that's listed as open but isn't. We build your hours in a way that's easy for you to update — no developer needed.

FAQ

Questions about restaurant website design

How much does a restaurant website cost in Australia?

A restaurant landing page starts from $600 AUD and a full website from $2,000 AUD — both fixed price. No ongoing fees to us. You pay once and the site is yours. Most restaurants choose the $2,000 package to get a full menu, gallery, and reservation system.

Can you build an online ordering system for my restaurant?

Yes. We can integrate with third-party ordering platforms (like Mr Yum, OrderUp, or Bopple) or build a custom ordering system from scratch. Custom ordering systems are typically an additional cost — let us know what you need and we'll quote accordingly.

Do I need to provide food photos?

We strongly recommend real photos of your food — they convert dramatically better than stock images. If you have a professional photographer, great. If not, even well-lit phone photos work well. We can advise on what shots you need before we build.

What reservation systems do you integrate with?

We integrate with OpenTable, Resy, ResDiary, Dimmi, and Quandoo — the most popular AU restaurant booking systems. For smaller venues that don't use a booking platform, we build a simple form that sends reservation requests straight to your email.
