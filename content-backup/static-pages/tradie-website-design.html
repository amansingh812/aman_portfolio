FAQ

Questions about tradie website design

How much does a tradie website cost in Australia?

At Build First Site, a tradie landing page starts from $600 AUD and a full business website from $2,000 AUD. Both are fixed price — you know the cost before we start, with no hourly billing or unexpected extras. Most tradies choose the $2,000 multi-page website to get full suburb SEO coverage and service pages.

What's the most important feature on a tradie website?

Click-to-call. On mobile, your phone number should be the first thing visible — ideally a sticky button at the top of every page. The second most important feature is your Google reviews, displayed prominently. People trust tradies who have reviews visible on their own website.

Will my website show up on Google for local searches?

We build every tradie site with on-page SEO: proper heading structure, LocalBusiness schema, sitemap submission, and suburb targeting pages. We also advise on your Google Business Profile, which drives the majority of local search calls for tradies — that's completely free and separate from your website.

Do I need to provide content and photos?

For photos — yes, even basic phone photos of your work make a huge difference in conversion. For written content — we handle it. We'll write your services, about section, and service area pages. You just tell us what you do and where you work.

What trades do you build websites for?

All of them. We've built for plumbers, electricians, landscapers, and general builders. Same great result for roofers, tilers, painters, cleaners, pest controllers, air conditioning technicians, and any other trade business in Australia.
