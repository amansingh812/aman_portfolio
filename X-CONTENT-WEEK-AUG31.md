## TUESDAY, SEP 1

### Post 1 — 7:30am (HOT TAKE — THREAD)
🧵 5 reasons your WordPress site is slower than it should be (and why switching isn't as scary as it sounds)

1/ WordPress wasn't built for 2026. It was built in 2003 for blogging. Every plugin you add is another PHP process, another database query, another attack surface.

2/ The average WP business site loads 15-20 plugins by year two. Each one is a black box someone else maintains — or doesn't.

3/ Modern stacks (Next.js, static generation, edge hosting) render pages before the visitor even asks for them. WordPress builds the page live, every single time, unless you bolt on caching plugins to fake it.

4/ Migrating doesn't mean starting from zero. Your content, your structure, your rankings — all portable. What changes is what's under the hood.

5/ If your site takes 4+ seconds to load on mobile, you're not losing rankings slowly — you're losing the visitor before the page even paints. Check yours free: buildfirstsite.com
