# Build First Site — Marketing Playbook (for a developer who hates marketing)

You don't need to "become a marketer." You need a simple weekly routine, and you let AI (me) write everything. Your job is to **send** and **show up to calls**. That's it.

---

## The truth about getting your first clients
Fast → slow, by how quickly they actually produce paying clients:

1. **Outbound (cold email / DM to local businesses)** — fastest. You contact businesses with bad/no websites.
2. **Freelance marketplaces (Upwork, Fiverr, Contra)** — builds reviews + cash while everything else warms up.
3. **Your network + referrals** — friends, ex-colleagues, anyone who owns/knows a business.
4. **Google Business Profile + directories (Clutch, DesignRush)** — inbound, free, slow-medium.
5. **Content / social (LinkedIn first)** — slow to pay off, but compounds. AI makes it nearly free to do.
6. **Google Ads** — fast but costs money; only after the contact form tracks conversions.

**Do #1 and #2 from day one. Do #3–5 in the background. #6 when you have budget.**

## Your weekly routine (≈30–45 min/day)
- **Mon–Fri:** Send **5 outbound messages** to local businesses (I write each one). = 25/week.
- **Mon / Wed / Fri:** Post **1 LinkedIn post** (I write it, you paste). 3/week.
- **Tue / Thu:** Apply to **3 relevant jobs** on Upwork/Contra (I write the proposal). 6/week.
- **Anytime:** Reply to any lead within an hour (I draft the reply).
- **Friday:** 15 min — tell me what happened, I adjust next week.

That's the whole machine. ~5 hours/week. Everything written for you.

## Channel by channel — what to do + how I help

### 1. Outbound (your #1 channel)
**Find targets:** local tradies, cafés, gyms, dentists, real estate agents with old/ugly/no websites (Google "plumber [suburb]", look for bad sites).
**The move:** email or DM the owner, point out one specific thing, offer a fix.
**I write:** the cold email, the follow-ups (most replies come from follow-up #2–3), and a "teardown" of their current site to attach. You just paste their business name.

### 2. Marketplaces (your #2 channel)
Upwork, Fiverr, Contra. Price LOW here only (to get first 3–5 reviews) — never on your own site.
**I write:** your profile bio, your service/gig descriptions, and a custom proposal for every job you apply to (tailored to the posting in 60 seconds).

### 3. Network & referrals
Tell everyone you build websites. Post it once. Ask 5 people if they know a business that needs one.
**I write:** the "I started a studio" announcement message + a referral ask.

### 4. Google Business Profile + directories
Free profiles that rank and send leads: Google Business Profile (service-area), Clutch, DesignRush, GoodFirms.
**I write:** every profile bio + your services list, optimized for search.

### 5. LinkedIn (your main content channel — start here, not Instagram)
LinkedIn is where businesses are. 3 posts/week. You don't need to be clever — share what you build.
**Post types I'll write for you:**
- "I rebuilt a [plumber]'s website in a week — here's the before/after" (your demo projects!)
- "Why most small-business sites lose customers (and the 3 fixes)"
- A short case study of Harbour Plumbing / Marlow & Vine
- A tip: "Your website's job in the first 5 seconds"
**I write:** every post, the hook, hashtags, and the first comment. You paste + add the image.

### 6. Instagram / X (optional, later)
Repurpose the LinkedIn posts as carousels/threads. I'll reformat them — zero extra thinking.
