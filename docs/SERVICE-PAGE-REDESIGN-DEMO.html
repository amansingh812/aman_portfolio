Questions people ask before hiring us

Can't see yours? Book a free call and ask the engineer directly.

How much does a website cost in Australia?

Our fixed prices start at $800 for 3–5 pages, $1,900 for up to 10 pages and $4,500 for an online store. Full cost guide →

How long does a website take to build?

A Starter site takes 5–7 days, a Business site 2–3 weeks and an online store 4–6 weeks.

Do I own the website?

Yes. The code, hosting account and domain are handed to you on completion.

Can you redesign my existing site without losing rankings?

Yes. We keep your URLs where possible and map 301 redirects where they change.

Can I update the content myself?

Yes. Text, images and blog posts are editable without code.

What does it cost to run after launch?

Hosting is often $0–40/month. Care + SEO is optional at $250/month. Hosting cost guide →
