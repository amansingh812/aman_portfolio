The problem

A working store that was losing sales in two places

Mobile Armour had a working store, but a slow one, and a catalogue big enough that shoppers struggled to find the right accessory for their exact device. Every extra second of load time and every dead-end search was a lost sale.

“Faster and smarter”: help customers find the right product instantly, and make the store feel quick on the phones they actually shop from.
Add from you: what the old store ran on (Shopify? WordPress?), roughly how many products, and one real symptom the owner complained about. Two sentences is enough.

Three shoppers, three fixes

Each part of the rebuild answers a real moment where the old store lost the sale.

The unsure shopper

“Will this fit my phone?”

Doesn’t know their exact model number and gives up on a long category list.

Fix: a shopping assistant that answers compatibility questions in plain language.
The browser

“Show me what goes with this”

Looking at a case, open to a screen protector or charger, but the old “related items” were static.

Fix: AI recommendations based on what they’re actually viewing.
The on-the-go buyer

“Just let me pay”

Shopping on a phone, on mobile data, with little patience for slow pages.

Fix: a faster front end and a short Stripe checkout built for mobile.
