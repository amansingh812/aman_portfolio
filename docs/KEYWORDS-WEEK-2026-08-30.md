## 2. What the data is actually telling us

### 2.1 "Affordable / cheap / inexpensive" is a whole cluster we do not serve

Four separate Low-competition keywords at 100–1k/mo, all the same intent, all
bidding $7.07–23.90 — advertisers pay real money for this traffic:

- affordable web design for small business
- affordable website design for small business
- inexpensive website design for small business
- cheap small business website design

**We are the single best-matched studio in Australia for this query and we have
no page for it.** Published $800 entry price, no discovery call, fixed quotes.
Every competitor hides pricing, so they cannot write this page honestly. We can.

This is the biggest gap in the list.

### 2.2 "Packages" is the same story

`small business web design packages` and `small business website design
packages`, both 100–1k, both Low. Our `/pricing/` page *is* a package table —
it just never uses the word "packages" in a title or H1.

That is a near-free win: an on-page fix, not a new page.

### 2.3 "Small business" beats "web design [city]"

Nearly every high-volume Low-competition term is phrased around **small
business**, not around a city. Our city pages target `web design perth` etc.,
which sit at Medium/High competition — that is why Perth has 534 impressions at
position 93.

We are not abandoning the city pages. But the `small business` framing is
demonstrably easier and we barely use it.

### 2.4 Google Business Profile terms are adjacent demand

`google page for my business` (100–1k, Low) and `google pages business` (100–1k,
Low) are people trying to get found on Google. They need what we sell, they just
do not know to search for a web designer yet. A guide here captures demand
upstream of the buying decision.

## 3. Action plan

### Priority 1 — the affordable cluster (this week)

**New page: `/affordable-website-design-small-business/`**

Targets four Low-competition 100–1k keywords at once. Content is already true,
which is the whole point:

- Why we can charge $800 when agencies charge $5,000+ (no office, no sales

  monthly platform fee

- What you actually get at each price point

Must state the price above the fold. That is the differentiator and the query.

## 4. Reality check on timing

None of this ranks without links. Search Console shows **0 external backlinks**.
These pages are the *right* targets and they will still sit on page 5 until
referring domains exist.

Sequence that actually works:

1. Ship the affordable page and the pricing fix — cheap, correct, done
2. Ship the cost calculator — the link-earning asset, and it serves this exact
   cluster
3. Directories and outreach for the first 10 referring domains
4. *Then* these keywords start moving

Building more pages ahead of step 3 produces more position-81 URLs.
