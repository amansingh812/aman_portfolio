# Competitor Deep Dive — Aug 2026

Three AU competitors analysed: **Havealook**, **Growth Digital**, **Aussify Web Design**.
Read alongside `CLAUDE.md` §4 (SEO strategy) and `docs/SEO-PLAYBOOK.md`.

---

## TL;DR — the five things that matter

1. **Every one of them leads with a price.** $995 / $1,000-off / $900. Our $600 landing page and
   $2,000 business site are competitive, but our pricing is buried — theirs is in the `<title>` tag.
2. **Havealook wins on review volume, not craft.** 4.8★ / 300 Google reviews, 231 Trustpilot.
   Their sites are hand-coded HTML on a proprietary CMS. We beat them technically and lose on trust.
3. **Aussify owns the niches we've ignored** — NDIS, medical, dental, healthcare. Low-competition,
   high-value, and they're ranking with thin pages. This is the biggest open gap.
4. **Growth Digital's whole site is one long landing page** on a GoHighLevel subdomain
   (`offer.growthdigital.com.au`). Near-zero SEO surface. They buy traffic, they don't earn it.
5. **All three inflate.** "50–249 employees", "10+ Shopify experts", "team of expert designers".
   Our honest two-person positioning is a real differentiator — but only if we make it visible.

## 1. Havealook — `havealook.com.au`

**The one to actually worry about.** 20 years old, ~80 indexed pages, mature internal linking.

### Positioning
> "Australian Web Design Agency & Custom Website Development — **From $995**"
> 3 week turnaround · Easy to Update Yourself · **100% Australian Owned** · 20+ Years' Experience

Their entire wedge is **"Australian owned, Australian support, not overseas."** One of their
featured Google reviews says it outright: *"their support based in Melbourne, yes Melbourne not
overseas."* This is aimed squarely at offshore studios — i.e. at us.

### Pricing (published, GST-inclusive, hosting included)

| Package | Price | Key inclusions |
|---|---|---|
| Starter | **$995** | 3 iStock images, CMS, hosting |
| Economy | **$1,495** | + slideshow, graphical buttons |
| Premium | **$1,995** | + full-width slideshow, sticky menu, custom footer |
| The Works | **$2,795** | + full-screen backgrounds, animation, logo preloader |
| Logo | **$295–395** | add-on |

Every tier includes hosting + GST + a 3-step process ("Pick a package → Send content → Launch")
and **3 rounds of changes**. No hidden fees messaging is aggressive: *"No hidden fees. No BS."*

### SEO structure — this is the playbook worth copying

- **City pages (5):** `/web-design-sydney`, `/melbourne-website-design`, `/brisbane-website-design`,
  `/web-design-adelaide`, `/cheap-website-design-perth` — note the **inconsistent URL patterns**,
  a legacy artifact, but each is a real page with unique copy.
- **Industry pages (3):** `/website-design-for-ndis-providers`, `/websites-for-doctors`,
  `/website-design-for-tradies`
- **Service pages (~15):** ecommerce (5 sub-pages!), CMS, SEO, SEM, PPC, WCAG accessibility,
  responsive, mobile, one-page design, logo
- **Modifier pages:** `/cheap-website-design-australia` — they target "cheap" head-on
- **Blog: ~40 posts**, heavily SEO-topic driven, with **named SEO case studies**
  (Premier Picket Fencing, Just Jeeps, McMahons Pty Ltd) — real client names, real results
- **Lead magnet:** a `/files/seo.pdf` titled "PAGE 1 GOOGLE"
- **Referral program** paying **up to $500** per conversion — a link-and-lead engine we don't have
- **Proprietary product ("InstaBlog")** — mobile blogging tool, gives them something to write about

### Trust layer
4.8★ / **300 Google reviews** · 231 Trustpilot reviews · "20,000+ websites built" · "15K+ customers"
· 1300 number · Melbourne GPO Box · form asks "How did you hear about us?" (attribution tracking)

### Weaknesses we can exploit
- **Technically dated.** They brag about "the fastest and purest form of coding which is HTML."
  No React, no Next.js, no AI. Static HTML on their own CMS.
- **You don't own the code.** Hosting + CMS are theirs. Leaving means rebuilding. Never stated.
- **Locked into their hosting** — recurring revenue disguised as a feature.
- **iStock stock photos as a headline package differentiator** (3 → 5 → 7 → 12 images) is a
  laughably weak value ladder in 2026.
- **Complaints exist** — ComplaintsBoard has a page for them.
- Inconsistent URL patterns, thin blog posts, no schema depth visible.

## 2. Growth Digital — `offer.growthdigital.com.au`

**Not really an SEO competitor.** This is a paid-traffic landing page, not a website.

### What they are
Shopify / Shopify Plus specialist, Melbourne-based. The URL is a **GoHighLevel-hosted subdomain**
(`assets.cdn.filesafe.space`, `leadconnectorhq.com` images) — a funnel page, not the main site.
Their real site is `growthdigital.com.au` (WordPress).

### Positioning
> "Australian Shopify Expert Designers & Developer — ★★★★★ 60+ Reviews"
> "High-Converting Shopify Stores Built for Growth at Affordable Price"

**Urgency/scarcity offer:** *"◆ $1,000 Off Shopify Packages ◆ Offer valid for the first 5 sign-ups
this month"* — repeated on a scrolling marquee, four times per row, multiple times down the page.

### Claimed stats (all unverifiable)
10+ Shopify experts · 20+ industries · 50+ 5-star reviews · 100+ stores · 20+ migrations ·
**4 weeks average build time**

### SEO surface: near zero
- Single page. All nav links are `#section-XXXX` anchors — no sub-pages at all.
- Zero real internal linking, no blog, no schema beyond basics.
- Their "city pages" are **six paragraphs on one page** (Melbourne, Sydney, Perth, Brisbane,
  Adelaide, Gold Coast) — that's not city SEO, that's keyword stuffing on a funnel page.
- Two typos visible in H4s: *"Shopify Agnecy"* ×2.
- Portfolio images all link to `#` — no case studies.

### What they do well
- **Conversion copy is sharp.** "A good-looking store attracts visitors; a well-built store creates
  customers." Every section ends in a CTA with a different verb: Get Instant Estimate → Request
  Quote → Get Shopify Proposal → Get Pricing → Free Proposal.
- **Three pricing models named without numbers:** Fixed Packages / Custom / Ad-Hoc hourly. They get
  the *transparency signal* without publishing a price. Clever, if less honest than ours.
- **8 full-text Google reviews** with real names, reproduced verbatim.
- **Industry list of 20 verticals** — cheap topical coverage.

### Takeaway
They are winning on **Google Ads + offer strength**, not organic. If you see them ranking, it's
paid. Don't chase them on SEO; do steal the offer mechanics.

## 3. Aussify Web Design — `aussifywebdesign.com.au`

**The most direct threat to our positioning** — and the most instructive.

### The setup
- Registered address: **Suite 836, Flinders Centre, 25 Restwell Street, Bankstown NSW 2200** —
  a serviced-office/mailbox address (same pattern as Dev Story's Pitt Street mailbox).
- Phone: **(02) 5839 5538** — a real-looking Sydney number.
- Clutch lists them as **founded 2019, "50–249 employees."** For a studio with 2 Clutch reviews.
- Trustpilot: **3.7 / 9 reviews.** Clutch: 4.5 / 2 reviews.
- Pricing structure ("Dedicated Project Manager" on every tier, "8 Dedicated Designers",
  "Multiple Revision Rounds", "Money Back Guarantee", "W3C Certified HTML", "48hrs TAT") is the
  **exact template used by offshore reseller agencies**. This is a white-label price list.

**They are almost certainly the thing they accuse us of being** — offshore, presenting as local.
The difference is we say so.

### Pricing (published — very close to ours)

|---|---|
| Basic Website (3 pages) | **$900** |
| Start Up (5 pages) | **$1,400** |
| Professional (5–10 pages, WordPress) | **$2,300** |

Note: they run a **monthly SEO retainer ladder** we don't have. That's recurring revenue and a

### SEO structure — where they're beating us

They have gone hard at **regulated / high-trust verticals**:

- `/ndis-website-design-australia`
- `/medical-website-design-australia`
- `/dental-clinic-website-design-australia`
- `/healthcare-website-design-services`
- `/shopify-website-design`, `/shopify-expert-sydney`
- `/amazon-marketing-agency`
- `/sydney-website-design`

And their blog is **listicle-bait aimed at buyers in the decision phase**:

- "Best NDIS Website Design Companies in Australia 2026"
- "10 Best Shopify Agencies in The Australia (2026)"
- "11 Best Healthcare Web Design Agencies in Sydney (2026)"
- "How to Choose the Best NDIS SEO Agency in Australia"
- "Website Design Platform Strategy for Picking NDIS Providers 2026"
- "Web Design for Doctors: 10 Best Tips for Australian Medical Practices"

**This is the single smartest thing any of the three are doing.** "Best X agencies in Australia
2026" posts rank for high-intent comparison searches, earn links from the agencies listed, and put
Aussify at #1 on their own list. Cheap to produce, disproportionately effective.

### Schema
They ship `WebSite` + `Organization` + `LocalBusiness` with geo-coordinates, opening hours, and
`sameAs` social profiles. Clean, but shallow — no `FAQPage`, no `Service`, no `BreadcrumbList`,
no `AggregateRating`.

### Weaknesses
- **Zero verifiable proof.** No stats on the homepage. No named clients. No case study depth.
- Portfolio page says "clients worldwide" — undercuts the "Aussie" brand.
- 3.7 Trustpilot is *below* threshold; buyers who check will bounce.
- Pricing pages are enormous walls of feature bullets (~75k chars) — terrible UX, and Google
  reads it as thin/templated.
- Package names are recycled nonsense ("Web Content Identity Package" = 1 article for $149;
  "Web Content Corporate Package" = the same thing for $129).

## What this changes about our plan

### Reordered priorities

**P0 — Trust layer (unchanged from CLAUDE.md, now urgent).**
Havealook's 300 reviews are the moat. Aussify's 2-review Clutch profile proves the *bar is low*:
Clutch Sydney lists firms with 2–3 reviews. Three real reviews from hsracegear, mobilearmour and
autozenlyai puts us on the board. **Nothing else we do matters as much as this.**

**P1 — Publish the price above the fold.**
All three lead with a number. Ours is stronger ($600 vs $900 vs $995) and we bury it. Put
"Websites from $600. Fixed price, in AUD." in the homepage H1 area *and* in the `<title>`.

**P2 — Take the verticals Aussify owns.**
`/ndis-website-design-australia/` is the standout: WCAG 2.1 AA is a legal requirement for NDIS
providers, we can actually build accessible Next.js sites, and Aussify's page is thin. Then
medical, then dental. These are low-volume/high-value — a $4k+ client, not a $600 one.

**P3 — Write the "Best X in Australia 2026" listicles.**
Copy Aussify's format, do it honestly:
- "Best Web Design Agencies in Sydney (2026): Honest Pricing Compared"
- "10 Best NDIS Website Designers in Australia (2026)"
- "Shopify Agencies in Australia: What They Actually Charge (2026)"
List real competitors with real published prices. This earns links (agencies link to lists they're
on), ranks for comparison intent, and our transparency is the hook.

**P4 — Add a monthly retainer product.**
Aussify's $225–1,499 SEO ladder and Havealook's SEO service are recurring revenue and a retention
mechanism. We have $150/mo maintenance — thin. Add an SEO tier.

**P5 — Referral program.**
Havealook pays $500. Cheapest link-and-lead channel available and we have nothing.

**P6 — Counter-positioning page: "Do you own your website?"**
Havealook's clients cannot leave — the CMS and hosting are Havealook's. This is never disclosed.
A page comparing "proprietary CMS vs code you own" targets `havealook alternative`,
`website platform lock-in australia`, and lands directly on their weak point.

### Copy angles worth stealing

- Havealook: *"No hidden fees. No BS. 3 easy steps."* — process clarity beats feature lists
- Havealook: the 4-question objection block (Cost? Fast? Easy? Quality?) — good structure
- Growth Digital: *"A good-looking store attracts visitors; a well-built store creates customers."*
- Growth Digital: varying the CTA verb per section rather than repeating "Contact us"
- Havealook: attribution field on the contact form ("How did you hear about us?")

### What NOT to do

- **Don't compete on "cheap."** Havealook owns `/cheap-website-design-australia` with 20 years of
  authority. We compete on *modern stack + you own it*, at a price that happens to be lower.
- **Don't inflate the team.** Aussify's "50–249 employees" and Growth Digital's "10+ experts" are
  exactly what makes them untrustworthy on inspection. Our two-person story is the asset.
- **Don't build the industry × city matrix.** Aussify has ~6 industry pages, not 300. Confirmed:
  depth beats volume here.
