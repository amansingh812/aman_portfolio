### The number that explains everything

| Position band | Impressions | Share |
|---|---|---|
| 1–10 (page 1) | 4 | **0.2%** |
| 11–20 (page 2) | 11 | 0.6% |
| 21–50 | 52 | 2.7% |

**98.4% of our impressions are on page 6 or deeper.** Nobody has ever
scrolled that far. This is not a traffic problem or a content problem — it is
a *ranking* problem, and ranking is decided almost entirely off-page.

### Trend: rising, not falling

First 14 days: 561 impressions. Last 14 days: 1,343. **+139%.**

The jump on 2–3 August (13 → 259/day) is Google discovering the deepened city
pages. Indexing works. Crawling works. Geo-targeting works — 84% Australian.
We are being served for the right queries in the right country and losing
every single one of them.

### The desktop skew is a symptom

Desktop 1,647 impressions (87%) vs mobile 251 (13%). For Australian local
service search you would expect the reverse — roughly 68% mobile. The
explanation is mechanical: mobile SERPs are shorter, so a page at position 90
never renders an impression on mobile at all. The desktop skew is just another
way of measuring how badly we rank.

  sitemaps accepted, fast builds. This is better than most AU agencies.

- Keyword targeting. Google is matching us to commercially relevant queries:
  Perth terms are 21% of impressions, cost/pricing terms 22%. Those are

### Not working — the entire off-page layer

- **Referring domains: effectively zero.** This is the binding constraint.
- **Google reviews: 0.** Havealook has ~300 at 4.8★.
- **Clutch profile: not live.**
- **Google Business Profile: no reviews feeding it.**

### The honest conclusion

**The on-page SEO work is finished. Arguably over-finished.** We have 37 pages
of genuinely good content sitting at position 83. Publishing page 38 produces
another position-83 URL. It does not move the first 37.

Everything from here is off-page or it is wasted effort.

## 5. Google Ads — the maths

`CLAUDE.md` says wait for 10 case studies and 10–20 reviews. Having now run the
numbers, that gate is close to right but for a different reason. Here is the
actual arithmetic.

### Real Australian CPCs, 2026

| Segment | Median CPC (AUD) |
|---|---|
| Australia, all industries | ~$3.81 |
| Marketing & Creative category | **$12.62** |
| "service + city" — Adelaide | $13.70 |
| "service + city" — Sydney | **$23.21** |

Market context: Australian click prices rose **31% between January and May
2026** and are up **21% year on year**, while search demand fell 23%. Ads are
getting more expensive and less abundant at the same time.

### What that means for a $1,900 product

Assume Perth/Adelaide at ~$15 CPC and a $1,500 test budget = **100 clicks.**

**Today — 0 reviews, no conversion tracking, no dedicated landing page:**

| Step | Rate | Result |
|---|---|---|
| Clicks | — | 100 |
| Landing conversion (no social proof) | 1–2% | 1–2 leads |
| Lead → client | ~25% | **0.25–0.5 clients** |
| Revenue | $1,900 each | **$475–950** |
| Spend | | **$1,500** |
| **Net** | | **Loss** |

**After 5+ reviews, a dedicated landing page and conversion tracking:**

| Step | Rate | Result |
|---|---|---|
| Landing conversion | ~4% | 4 leads |
| Lead → client | ~25% | **1 client** |
| Revenue | | **$1,900** |
| Spend | | **$1,500** |
| **Net** | | **+$400 — thin but viable** |

### The real conclusion about Ads

**At $15–23 CPC, Google Ads barely works for a $1,900 website and never works
for the $800 starter package.** One client at $1,900 costs roughly $1,500 to
acquire. That is not a business.

Where the maths does work:

- **E-commerce builds at $4,500** — same acquisition cost, 2.4× the revenue.
- **Retainers.** A $150–500/month retainer compounds; lifetime value over two
  years dwarfs the click cost.
- **Long-tail, low-competition terms** rather than "web design perth".

### When to start — three gates, all three required

1. **5+ Google reviews live.** Without social proof, paid traffic converts at
   1–2% and the spend is simply donated.
2. **A dedicated conversion landing page** — not the homepage. One offer, one
   form, price visible, reviews visible, no navigation.
3. **Conversion tracking configured** in GA4 and Google Ads. Running ads
   without knowing which keyword produced the lead is gambling.

Realistically: **October–November 2026**, and even then start at **$500–800/month
on Perth + cost-intent long-tail only**, selling the $4,500 e-commerce build or
a retainer — not the $800 package.

Until those gates are met, that money buys directory listings, tools, and
case studies that keep working for years. Growth Digital is the cautionary
example: one funnel page, zero organic asset, permanent ad spend.
