# SERP Analysis — What Google's Top Pages Are Actually Doing
**Keywords analysed:** `tradie website` · `web design adelaide` · `squarespace vs custom website`  
**Date:** 10 August 2026  
**Method:** Fetched and read the top 3–5 ranking pages for each keyword.

---

## How to read this document

For each keyword, I pulled the pages Google currently ranks #1–5, read their full content,
and extracted every pattern that appears in 2+ of those pages. These are not opinions — they
are what Google has decided is the correct format for each topic. If you match or beat these
patterns with better content, you rank. If you don't, you don't.

## Keyword 1 — `tradie website` (5,000/mo, Low competition)

**Top ranking pages:** customwebcreations.com.au · corewebhub.com.au · dmdesignstudio.au ·
websitebuilderaustralia.com.au · visualweb.com.au · budgetbyte.com.au

### Patterns every ranking page uses

**Title format:** `[Action Phrase] Web Design for Tradies: [Year] Guide to [Outcome]`
- "Best Tradie Websites Australia: A 2026 Design Guide"
- "Web Design for Tradies: The Ultimate 2026 Guide"
- "Choose the Right Web Design for Tradies: 2026 Guide to Winning More Jobs"

**The core angle Google rewards:** Lead generation tool — not a digital business card.
Every ranking page opens with a variation of "your tradie website should be generating
leads 24/7, not just existing." This is the frame. Open with it.

**Content sections all ranking pages include:**
1. Why a tradie website is different from a regular business site
2. Mobile-first — the "mid-emergency" argument (70%+ of tradie searches are mobile)
3. Must-have features: click-to-call, quote form (max 3 fields), project photo gallery, Google reviews widget
4. Local SEO section — suburb targeting, "Plumber Moonee Ponds" beats "Plumber Melbourne"
5. Pricing in AUD — every page mentions $3,000–$8,000 for professional builds, and $599–$1,200 for budget
6. Real photos argument — real project photos beat stock images every time
7. FAQ — 5–8 questions, all answered in 100–200 words each

**Images every ranking page has:**
- Before/after website screenshots (old vs new)
- Mobile mockup showing the site on a phone
- Screenshot of a tradie's Google Maps/GBP listing
- Real work photos of the tradie's projects (plumbing, electrical, building)

**Word count:** 1,800–2,500 words for blog posts; 1,200–1,600 for service pages

**CTA pattern:** Primary CTA above the fold + CTA after every H2 section + sticky mobile CTA bar

### What our `/tradie-website-design/` page is missing

- H1 says "Tradie Website Design Australia" — the keyword is just "tradie website". Change the H1.
- No mobile mockup image
- No "mid-emergency" mobile angle
- No visible AUD pricing table on the page
- No real project photo examples
- No local SEO section (suburb targeting advice)
- No FAQ section with visible Q&As (we have schema but no visible content — Google needs both)
- Content is likely under 1,000 words — needs to reach 1,500+

## Keyword 2 — `web design adelaide` (5,000/mo, Low competition)

**Top ranking pages:** webadelaide.com.au · adelaidewebdesigner.com.au (300+ reviews) ·
studio60design.com.au · boylen.com.au (30 years) · whitepeakdigital.com (91 reviews)

### Patterns every ranking page uses

**H1:** Dead simple exact match. Every single #1–5 page uses "Web Design Adelaide" as the H1.
No clever wording, no taglines in the H1. Just the keyword.

**Trust signals — above the fold, before any copy:**
- Google star rating + review count (adelaidewebdesigner.com.au: 300+ reviews, 5.0)
- White Peak Digital: "91 Google reviews" in the hero section
- Not below the fold. Not in a testimonials section. Right at the top.

**Stats displayed as large numbers:**
- White Peak Digital: "1M website visitors for clients this year"
- "430% SEO Return on Investment" (with named client)
- These are displayed as hero callouts, not buried in body text

**Team section — named people with photos and job titles:**
White Peak Digital names 8 staff members with photos. Adelaidewebdesigner.com.au has 8 departments.
This is a trust signal Google values: real people = real business.

**Case studies with client logos and specific outcomes:**
- Not "we've worked with many clients" — actual logos: World Gym, Griffith University, Flinders University
- Each case study has a specific metric: "430% SEO ROI", "significant uplift in conversion rates"
- Named testimonials with company and person name, 50–100 words each
- 8–12 testimonials per page

**Adelaide-specific content:**
- Named Adelaide suburbs and business districts
- "South Australia" used throughout (not just Adelaide)
- Local business examples from recognisable SA companies
- Mentions Adelaide's business landscape, mining/defence sector

**Platform logos:**
- WordPress logo + 1-line description
- Shopify logo + 1-line description
- WooCommerce logo + 1-line description
This says "we have options" and targets people searching for those specific platforms.

**FAQ section — 7–8 questions, always includes:**
1. How much does a website cost? (→ mentions AUD pricing range)
2. Why is custom better than a website builder?
3. How long does it take?
4. What CMS is best for ecommerce?
5. Can you help if I'm not in Adelaide?

**Urgency tactic:**
"Our project intake window is closing before end of August 2026."
This is likely perpetually updated. You can use a genuine version: "Currently booking projects
from [next month onwards]."

**AI visibility section (new in 2026):**
White Peak Digital has links at the bottom: "Ask AI about White Peak Digital" → ChatGPT / Google /
Claude / Perplexity. This is a brand-authority signal — linking to AI summaries of your business
is starting to appear on top-ranking AU agency pages.

**Word count:** 2,500–4,000 words. Long. Comprehensive.

### What our `/web-design-adelaide/` page is missing

- Reviews above the fold (we have 0 reviews — this is the critical gap)
- Named team members with photos
- Adelaide-specific business examples and suburb references
- AUD pricing visible on the page ("typically $800–$3,500")
- Case study thumbnails with client names and real results
- Platform logos (WordPress, Next.js, React)
- Intake urgency CTA
- FAQ section visible on page (minimum 6 questions)
- Word count is likely too low — needs to reach 2,000+ words

## Keyword 3 — `squarespace vs custom website` (position 45 — closest to page 1)

**Top ranking pages:** mywebpro.com.au · velacore.au · stratifyiq.com.au · websitebuilderaustralia.com.au

### Patterns every ranking page uses

**Two distinct formats:**

**Format A — Short punchy post (4 min, 800–1,000 words):**
Used by mywebpro.com.au. Structure:
- Punchy intro that directly challenges the platform's "cheap" pitch
- One cost table (Squarespace hidden costs breakdown in AUD)
- "But cost isn't the whole story" section
- Simple two-column comparison (template vs custom)
- Closing question to the reader ("Is your website a placeholder or your best salesperson?")
- No FAQ. No images beyond a hero.

**Format B — Comprehensive guide (12 min, 2,500–3,500 words):**
Used by velacore.au. Structure:
- "What are we comparing?" section first (defines terms for beginners)
- Feature-by-feature comparison (ease, cost, SEO, flexibility, security, scalability)
- Multiple AUD pricing tables with real numbers
- "Business stage framework" — which platform for which stage (BRILLIANT for featured snippets)
- "Hidden costs nobody tells you" section
- At-a-glance summary table
- "When you've outgrown your platform" section
- FAQ (5 questions)
- Multiple AI-generated images between sections

**The featured snippet magnet — every ranking page has a comparison table:**

| Feature | Squarespace | Custom Website |
|---|---|---|
| Setup | Easy | Handled for you |
| Monthly Cost (AUD) | $17–$69 | Varies |
| SEO Control | Good basics | Fully custom |
| Customisation | Limited | Unlimited |

Google extracts these tables for featured snippets. Our page needs one.

**Images on ranking posts:**
- AI-generated illustrations (every ranking blog post uses them now)
- Infographic comparing the platforms visually
- A "business stage journey" illustration
- Screenshots of each platform's interface

**AUD pricing specificity:**
Every ranking page includes specific AUD numbers: "$33–$49/month", "$3,000–$8,500 to build",
"$9,140–$20,340 over 5 years". Vague posts don't rank. Specific numbers do.

**Internal linking pattern:**
Velacore links to related posts within the article body (not just the footer):
- "website redesign costs" → in-article link
- "signs website is costing you customers" → in-article link
This is topical cluster building in action.

### What our `/squarespace-vs-custom-website/` page needs (it's at position 45 — closest to page 1)

- Add the comparison table as a proper HTML table (featured snippet target)
- Add AUD 5-year cost breakdown (specific numbers, not ranges)
- Add a "Who should use Squarespace" vs "Who should use custom" section
- Add 5 FAQ items with schema
- Add 2–3 AI-generated illustrations between sections
- Add internal links to `/pricing/`, `/blog/website-cost-australia/`, and `/wix-vs-custom-website/`
- Push word count to 1,500+ (currently likely ~800)
- Add "2026" to the title

## What competitors have that we genuinely cannot fake

**Google reviews.** This is the #1 difference between ranking and not ranking for city pages.
adelaidewebdesigner.com.au has 300+. White Peak Digital Adelaide has 91. We have 0.
This alone explains why their pages rank at position 5–10 and ours rank at position 89–93.

**Fix:** Get reviews from HS Race Gear, Mobile Armour, Autozenly, and LT Finance.
Even 4 reviews at 5 stars is a start. You cannot out-content a 300-review competitor
on a local service page — but 4 real reviews is better than 0, and it moves the needle.

**Genuine case studies with client logos and outcomes.**
"We built a website for a client" is not a case study. "We rebuilt Mobile Armour's site
and their product page load time dropped from 6.2s to 0.8s — they now rank #1 for
mobile phone screen repair Melbourne" is a case study. Get the real numbers.
