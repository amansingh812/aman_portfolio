## 3. Content — nine formats that need no face

Screen recordings only, all of these:

| # | Format | Length | Why it works |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Lighthouse reveal** | 15s | Run PageSpeed on a build, show the score hitting green. Proves the performance claim instead of stating it. |
| 2 | **Side-by-side load race** | 20s | Your build vs a typical page-builder site, loading simultaneously. Wordless and obvious. |
| 3 | **Scroll-through** | 25s | Slow scroll of a finished build, phone frame. Let the work speak. |
| 4 | **Code to live** | 20s | Split screen: editor on one side, live site on the other, one change appearing. |
| 5 | **The price scroll** | 15s | Screen-record scrolling your own pricing page. Nobody else in this market can post this. |
| 6 | **Anonymous teardown** | 30s | PageSpeed on a genuinely slow site with the name and URL blurred, then the three things wrong. **Never name the business.** |
| 7 | **Before / after** | Carousel | Old site → new site, 6–8 frames. The single highest-performing format in this niche. |
| 8 | **"You don't need me if…"** | Carousel | Honest disqualification. Travels further than a sales pitch and filters your enquiries. |
| 9 | **The five-year number** | Carousel | Build price + monthly fee × 60. Straight from the affordable page. |

### Three pillars, roughly 50 / 30 / 20

- **50% proof** — formats 1, 2, 3, 4, 7. The work, on screen.
- **30% price transparency** — formats 5, 9. Your structural advantage.
- **20% honest advice** — formats 6, 8. Builds trust, filters buyers.

### Rules for every post

- First person. "I built this", not "we deliver solutions."
- Spec builds (`harbour-plumbing`, `marlow-vine`, `aurelia-estates`) **must be
  labelled concept builds** on screen, every time.
- No invented metrics. If you don't have the number, don't imply one.
- No headcount, no implied Melbourne team. You are the engineer; your brother is
  the local contact. That is the honest and the more persuasive story.
- Never publicly name a business whose site you are criticising.

## 5. What to expect, honestly

**Months 1–3: no enquiries.** That is normal and not a sign it is failing. What
you are buying in that window is the thing that happens *after* someone finds
you on Google — they check LinkedIn and Instagram, see real work and a real
person, and believe you exist. That is a conversion asset, not an acquisition
channel.

Social will not fix the ranking problem. They are separate channels with
separate timelines.

**Before you post anything: get the four client reviews.** Content pointing at a
site with zero social proof converts badly. Reviews remain the #1 blocker and no
amount of posting substitutes for them.

### What to measure

Not followers. Track: profile → website clicks, DMs that mention a project, and
"how did you hear about us" on the contact form. Review at **21 Dec 2026** — a
full quarter. Anything shorter is noise.
