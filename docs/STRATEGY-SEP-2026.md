# Strategy — September 2026

Competitor deep-dive, customer-eye review of our own site, and a plan split by
who does what. Written 11 Sep 2026 from live SERP data and live competitor
pages, not from memory.

---

## 0. The finding that changes the plan

**We are not the cheap option. We assumed we were.**

I searched the exact term we just built a page for — *affordable website design
small business Australia fixed price* — and pulled the actual providers ranking
for it:

| Provider | Entry price | Model |
|---|---|---|
| **The Affordable Websites** | **$399** | WordPress / Elementor |
| Blend Designs | $999 | Custom one-pager |
| Eoan Technologies | $1,500 | 5-page starter |
| **Build First Site (us)** | **$800** | Next.js custom |
| Pink Fig Creative | $1,999 | Squarespace, "launched in 1 day" |
| EG Solutions | $3,500 + GST | Custom WordPress |

We sit **fourth of six on price**. `/affordable-website-design-small-business/`
is currently arguing a position we cannot actually hold — someone comparing us
against the top result sees $800 against $399 and we lose that comparison
outright.

**This does not mean drop the page.** It means change what "affordable" argues.
More on that in §3.

## 1. The main competitor, examined properly

**theaffordablewebsites.au** — ranks #3 for our target term, 12+ years, claims
2,000 Australian clients.

### What they genuinely do better than us

| | Them | Us |
|---|---|---|
| Price in the H1 | **"Affordable Websites Sydney From $399"** | Price in title, not H1 |
| City pages | **8** (Syd, Melb, Bris, Perth, Adel, Darwin, Canberra, Tas) | 5 |
| Primary CTA | **`tel:` link — phone, in the hero** | Form → contact page |
| Niche pages | NDIS, cleaning company | NDIS, dental, tradie, restaurant, real estate |
| Pricing tiers shown | **4, on the homepage** | 6 |
| Trust wedge | **"100% AUSTRALIAN OWNED AND OPERATED"** | direct access to the builder |

Three of those are straightforward for us to copy and two we should not.

**The phone CTA is the one that matters most.** Their hero button dials a number.
Ours opens a form. For tradies, cafés and clinics — our stated market — phone
beats form by a wide margin, and we have already identified twice that our
number appears only in the footer.

### Where they are weak, and it is exploitable

Read their homepage carefully and it falls apart:

- **Four different starting prices on one page.** Title says `$399`. Hero
  heading says `$499`. Body says `$599`. FAQ says `$599`. A buyer who notices
  this stops trusting the number entirely.
- **Three different tenure claims.** "over 20 years", "over 12 years", "over 10
  years" — all on the same page.
- **`0 +` Satisfied Customers.** A broken counter rendering literal zero, sitting
  under a heading claiming 2,000 clients.
- **Built on WordPress + Elementor**, which is precisely the stack we argue
  against — and their own site is the evidence.

That is our opening, and it is not "we are cheaper". It is **our numbers are
consistent and checkable and theirs are not.**

### The "100% Australian owned" wedge

Both they and Havealook lead with it. It is aimed squarely at operations like
ours. We cannot and must not counter it with a claim of our own (CLAUDE.md §1 —
never imply an Australia-only operation).

The honest counter is a different axis: **"Australian owned" tells you who banks
the money. It does not tell you who writes your code, whether the site will be
fast, or whether you can leave.** We answer all three in writing. They answer
none of them.

## 2. Our site, read as a customer

I went through the homepage, `/pricing/`, `/web-design-perth/` and
`/affordable-website-design-small-business/` as a business owner would.

### What genuinely lands

- **Price visible before any conversation.** Still our sharpest asset. Most
  competitors either hide it or contradict themselves on it.
- **The reviews carousel.** Seven named reviews, each linked to a live site the
  visitor can open. Far stronger than a logo wall.
- **Real client work on real domains.** hsracegear.com, mobilearmour.com.au,
  banishshoes.com — a sceptic can speed-test them personally.
- **"Are you based in Perth? No."** Answering the objection before it is raised
  builds more trust than a fake local address.

### What is costing us conversions

**1. No phone number above the fold.** The single biggest gap. Local service
buyers call. Ours is footer-only while the top competitor puts a dial button in
the hero.

**2. The homepage hero image is a woman in a VR headset.** We sell websites to
tradies, cafés and clinics. VR signals "tech startup", not "someone who will
build my plumbing website". It is the first thing a visitor sees and it speaks
to the wrong buyer.

**3. Six pricing tiers.** They show four. Choice paralysis is real, and our
Application and Custom Software tiers are irrelevant to the small business
segment we are targeting.

**4. "Book a free call" jumps to Calendly on another domain** at the exact
moment of conversion.

**5. `Accepting new projects — Q3 2026`** — Q3 ends this month. A stale badge
reads worse than no badge.

**6. Landing pages carry no proof.** `/web-design-perth/` makes claims about
price, speed and ownership with no screenshot, no example, no link to the
portfolio. Every assertion, nothing to verify.

## 3. Where we are actually losing

Not on quality. On four specific things:

| Gap | Reality |
|---|---|
| **Authority** | 0 referring domains. 70% of impressions sit at position 80+. |
| **Reviews** | 0 Google reviews. Havealook has 300. |
| **Price position** | $800 vs $399 — we are not the value pick and should stop implying it |
| **Conversion path** | No phone CTA, no proof on landing pages, offsite booking |

Authority and reviews are the long game and both are parked or pending.
**Price position and conversion path are fixable this week and cost nothing.**

---

## 4. The strategic call: stop arguing "cheap"

At $800 against a $399 competitor, "affordable" is a fight we lose. But the
$399 build is WordPress/Elementor, template-based, on their hosting.

So the argument changes from **cheap** to **cheapest to own over five years**:

> $399 buys you a template on someone else's platform. In three years you are
> paying to rebuild because you cannot leave. Our $800 buys code in your name,
> free hosting, and no exit cost. Five years in, the $399 site has cost more.

That argument is true, it is checkable, and they cannot make it back at us —
their own site is the WordPress build we are describing.

**Action:** rework `/affordable-website-design-small-business/` from "we are
cheap" to "cheap is a five-year number, not a sticker price". The five-year cost
table already on the page becomes the lead argument rather than a supporting
section.

## 6. What we measure

Site-average position is the wrong instrument — it is impression-weighted, and
70% of our impressions come from head terms at position 80+. Publishing more
content mathematically pushes it the wrong way, because every new query enters
at 90.

Track these four instead:

| Metric | Now | 90-day target |
|---|---|---|
| **Referring domains** | 0 | 10+ |
| **Google reviews** | 0 | 5+ |
| **Impressions in top 20** | 26 of 8,077 | 300+ |
| **Position on 10 named target keywords** | — | establish baseline, then track |

The named-keyword basket is the honest version of "are we ranking": `tradie
website`, `ndis website`, `web designers perth wa`, `affordable web design for
small business`, `small business website checklist`, `web design adelaide`,
`website design quote`, `small business website redesign`, `web developer
canberra`, `how much does a website cost australia`.

**Retracted:** the earlier "average position under 50 in 90 days" target. It
assumed 10+ referring domains in the same window. Links are parked, so the
target is not reachable and should not stand.

## 7. The honest summary

The website is better built than every competitor examined. That is not the
constraint and has not been for months.

We lose on three things: **nobody links to us, nobody has reviewed us, and the
site does not make it easy enough to phone.** The first two are slow. The third
takes an afternoon.

The competitor ranking above us for our own target keyword has four different
prices on one page, three different tenure claims, and a customer counter
showing zero. They rank anyway — because they have twelve years of links and we
have none.

That is the whole gap, stated plainly.

advantage exists.
