## Read this first — two honest caveats

**1. There are no search volumes in this document.** We do not have a keyword
tool connected. Every number you see in a competitor's blog post is guessed or
scraped, and inventing them here would make this list feel authoritative when
it isn't. **Before committing to any keyword below, validate it in Google
Keyword Planner** — free with any Google Ads account, no spend required. Thirty
minutes of that beats any assumption in this file.

What this list *is* based on: keywords our competitors actively target (proven
commercial demand — nobody builds a page for a term nobody searches), our own
existing pages, and the buying journey.

**2. I was wrong about NDIS being an easy win.** In the earlier plan I said
"Aussify ranks there with a thin page." That was incomplete. There is a whole
ecosystem of **NDIS-only web design agencies** with exact-match domains —
`ndiswebsitedesigns.com.au`, `participantconnect.com.au`, `velacore.au`,
`2brownies.com.au` (claiming 200+ NDIS sites built), plus SoDutch and
AusAsiaOnline running dedicated NDIS pages.

The market is real — 19,498 registered providers. But `ndis website design` as
a head term is defended by specialists, not by generalists with thin pages.
**We do not win that term head-on. We win the long tail underneath it**, which
is where our actual capability edge sits.

## How the tiers work

| Tier | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Money** | Person is ready to hire. Highest value, hardest to rank. |
| **Research** | Person is deciding how/what to buy. Winnable, feeds Money pages. |
| **Problem** | Person has a symptom, not a solution yet. Easiest to rank, slowest to convert. |

Ship **Research** keywords first. Money terms need authority we don't have yet;
Problem terms convert too slowly to justify going first.

## 2. NDIS cluster — the long tail, not the head term

**Do not target `ndis website design` first.** Exact-match-domain specialists
own it. Target the questions they answer badly, where our WCAG capability is a
genuine and provable differentiator.

| Keyword | Tier | Why us |
|---|---|---|
| ndis website accessibility requirements | Research | **Best entry point.** Compliance question, not a sales question. We can genuinely answer it. |
| wcag 2.1 aa compliance australia | Research | Technical, low competition, positions the capability |
| is my ndis website accessible | Problem | Feeds an accessibility-checker tool |
| ndis provider website requirements | Research | Regulatory framing, high trust |
| accessible website design australia | Research | Broader than NDIS, same capability |
| ndis website design cost | **Money** | Only after the above rank |
| ndis website design australia | **Money** | Head term — last, not first |
| website for ndis participants | Research | Participant-side, underserved angle |
| ndis provider seo | Research | Adjacent service |

**The wedge:** WCAG 2.1 AA is a legal obligation for NDIS providers, most
agencies cannot actually deliver it, and we can. Lead with the compliance
question and let the commercial terms follow. An accessibility-checker tool
would be the single most linkable asset in this cluster.

## Priority order

**Weeks 1–5 — expand what exists.** The five thin posts, one per week. Existing
URLs, existing history, fastest return. Start with `tradie-website-cost`.

**Weeks 6–8 — the NDIS long tail.** `ndis website accessibility requirements`
first, then `wcag 2.1 aa compliance australia`. Compliance angle only. No
commercial NDIS terms yet.

**Weeks 9–12 — comparison content.** `havealook alternative` and
`do you own your website`. Cheap to rank, high intent, hits a competitor's
weak point.

**Ongoing — one tool.** Speed checker or accessibility checker. This is the
piece that lifts everything else, because without links nothing here ranks
regardless of quality.

   from established agencies, pick a longer-tail variant.

   `public/sitemap.xml` first.
