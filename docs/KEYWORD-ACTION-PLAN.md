# Keyword Action Plan — Build First Site
**Data source:** Google Ads Keyword Planner + Google Search Console · Exported 10 Aug 2026  
**Scope:** Australia-wide + 6 cities (Melbourne, Sydney, Brisbane, Perth, Adelaide, Canberra)

---

## The honest picture first

GSC shows **0 clicks** across all 11 indexed pages. Every page sits between position 64 and 100
(pages 7–10). The site is indexed — Google knows it exists — but nothing is ranking yet.

That is normal for a site this young. The job now is not to write more pages. It is to make
the pages that exist rank, and to build a handful of new ones where the keyword opportunity
is genuinely wide open.

### Why the city pages rank at position 89–93

They exist but are thin. Google can see the page, reads a few paragraphs, and decides 20 other
sites have more helpful content about web design in that city. The fix is depth, not a new page:
more specific content about that city, local business problems, AUD pricing, local examples.

### Action items — Tier 1

**Week 1: Tradie cluster (biggest ROI)**
- `/tradie-website-design/` — add primary keyword "tradie website" to H1, title, and first paragraph.
  Currently targets "tradie website design" (a variant). 5k/mo is on the head term.
- Add a proper content body: average job size for a plumber/electrician/builder site, what
  features matter (click-to-call, quote form, Google reviews widget, photo gallery, maps),
  real AUD pricing table. Target 1,500+ words.
- Internal link from `/blog/tradie-website-cost/` to the service page with anchor text "tradie website".

**Week 2: Adelaide + Perth depth**
- `/web-design-adelaide/` — add: why Adelaide businesses have a different brief (smaller market,
  more referral-driven), AUD pricing, service industries common in Adelaide (health, mining,
  defence), FAQ schema with 5 Adelaide-specific questions.
- `/web-design-perth/` — same treatment. Perth is FIFO-economy driven — mention that.
  "web designers perth wa" uses the state abbreviation, so include "WA" in the copy.

**Week 3: NDIS hub**
- Create `/ndis-website-design/` as a hub page (the landing page). The 4 blog posts become
  cluster content linking into it. Without a hub, the keyword "ndis website" has no single
  strong page to rank — the authority is split across 4 URLs.

  performing content type on the site. Write more comparisons.

## What not to do

- **Do not write "what is web design" or "why does my business need a website"** — nobody
  commercial searches those. You want people with credit cards, not curiosity.
- **Do not create city × industry matrix pages** (e.g. "web design for tradies in perth").
  That path creates 50 thin pages and risks a scaled-content-abuse penalty.
- **Do not target High competition terms** without reviews, backlinks, and case studies.
  You cannot outrank agencies with 300 Google reviews on "web design australia" right now.
- **Do not start Google Ads** until you have 10+ reviews and a dedicated landing page.
  At $5.50/day the algorithm never learns.

## One metric to watch every Monday

Open Search Console → Performance → Last 28 days → Pages tab.

The number to move: **average position across all pages**. Right now it is ~85.
Target: below 50 in 4 weeks, below 20 in 8 weeks, page 1 on at least 3 terms in 12 weeks.

When a page crosses position 20, that is the signal to add more internal links pointing to it,
build one external link (directory, guest post, client backlink), and optimise the meta title
for click-through rate.
