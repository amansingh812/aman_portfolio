Before using any of these on the site
1. Download it and store it in public/assets/imgs/blog/<slug>/ as WebP (~1200px, under 150 KB). Never hotlink.
2. Give it a descriptive filename and alt text, and note the Unsplash link and date in docs/IMAGE-LICENCES.md.
3. Photos with people: fine as scene-setting, but never caption them as a client, staff member or testimonial.
4. Stock is the filler. Real screenshots of client sites and our own charts still do more for ranking and trust.
