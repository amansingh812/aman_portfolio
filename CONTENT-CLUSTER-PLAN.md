# Build First Site — Content Cluster Plan

A topic cluster is a group of interlinked articles around one theme. Google reads the internal links between them as "this site is an authority on websites for Australian small businesses" — which is what makes a *new* domain rank faster. Everything links to and from the **pillar page** (the cost guide).

**Pillar page:** `/blog/website-cost-australia/` — *How much does a website cost in Australia?* (expanded to ~2,550 words, 15 sections, 8 FAQs). Every article below links up to it; it links down to them.

## How the cluster links together (the important part)

```
                    ┌─────────────────────────────┐
                    │  PILLAR: Website Cost AU     │
                    │  /blog/website-cost-australia│
                    └──────────────┬──────────────┘
         ┌───────────┬─────────────┼─────────────┬───────────┐
         ▼           ▼             ▼             ▼           ▼
   WordPress vs   How long to   Tradie cost   Restaurant   Ecommerce
     custom        build                        cost         cost
         │           │             │             │           │
         └───────────┴──────► all link back up to pillar ◄───┘
                             + across to each other
                             + down to city pages + /#contact
```

Rule for every new article: link **up** to the pillar, **across** to 2 sibling articles, and **out** to a city page or the contact form. That's already done in the 5 live articles.

## Publishing cadence

- **1 article per week** is plenty. Consistency beats volume.
- I draft → you review → you deploy. ~15 min of your time per article.
- Re-touch the pillar page every 4–6 weeks (update prices, add a FAQ) — freshness is a ranking signal.
